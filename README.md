# Laptop Sales Data Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a sales dataset containing 2,000 sales transactions to identify sales trends, revenue performance, customer behavior, product performance, and regional differences.

The project was created as part of my Data Analytics portfolio to demonstrate my ability to work with real-world business data, perform data analysis, and communicate insights using data.

## Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance
- Identify the best-performing product categories
- Compare revenue across different regions
- Analyze sales performance by sales channel
- Compare new and returning customers
- Examine the relationship between quantity, price, discounts, and revenue
- Identify patterns that could support business decision-making

## Tools Used

- **Microsoft Excel** – Data analysis and visualization
- **Pivot Tables** – Summarizing sales performance
- **Charts & Dashboards** – Visualizing key findings
- **Data Cleaning** – Preparing the dataset for analysis

## Key Analysis

### Revenue Performance

The dataset generated approximately **$2.45 million in total revenue** across 2,000 orders.

### Product Categories

The **Computers** category generated the highest revenue, followed by:

1. Computers
2. Monitors
3. Networking
4. Storage
5. Accessories
6. Office Supplies

### Regional Performance

Revenue was compared across five regions:

- North
- Central
- East
- South
- West

The **North region** generated the highest revenue in the dataset.

### Sales Channels

The analysis compared four sales channels:

- Online
- Corporate Sales
- Retail Store
- Marketplace

The **Online channel** generated the highest revenue.

### Customer Analysis

Returning customers generated significantly more revenue than new customers, highlighting the importance of customer retention and repeat purchases.

## Dashboard

The project includes an Excel dashboard designed to provide a visual overview of:

- Total Revenue
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Product Category
- Sales by Sales Channel
- Customer Type Performance
- Sales Trends

## Key Insights

Some of the main findings from the analysis include:

- Computers were the strongest product category by revenue.
- Online sales generated the highest revenue among the sales channels.
- The North region recorded the highest revenue.
- Returning customers contributed substantially more revenue than new customers.
- Revenue performance varies considerably across products, regions, and sales channels.
